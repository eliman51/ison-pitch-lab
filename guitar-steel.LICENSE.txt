guitar-steel.m4a
----------------

The steel-string guitar notes the Guitar lab plays. 17 notes of the "FSS Steel-String Acoustic Guitar" sound bank
(release 2020-05-21) of the FreePats project, which was assembled by roberto@zenvoid.org from the "FS Seagull Steel
String Acoustic Guitar" samples by Gary Campion of FlameStudios.

    https://freepats.zenvoid.org/Guitar/steel-acoustic-guitar.html

Changed for this app on 23 November 2026 by tools/guitarpack.py: 17 of the loud-layer notes (HV_40, 43, 45, 48, 50,
52, 55, 58, 61, 64, 66, 68, 71, 74, 76, 79, 82) were cut to start at the pluck, shortened to at most 4.2 seconds,
set to the same loudness, joined into one file and compressed to AAC. The uncompressed originals are at the address
above; tools/guitarpack.py rebuilds this file from them.
